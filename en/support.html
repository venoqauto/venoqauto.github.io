Frequently asked

Where is my data kept?

On your device. There is no server it is transferred to. See the privacy notice for details.

How do I back up my data?

The settings include a full backup that also contains all images. You store the file wherever you like, for example in iCloud Drive or on an external disk, and you can encrypt it with a password. Keep that password safe — without it the backup cannot be opened again.

Can I switch between iPhone and Mac?

Yes. The devices reconcile through a shared folder. One rule: work on one device until you have transferred, otherwise an older state will overwrite newer changes.

Does the app replace my tax adviser?

No. The reports help you keep track and prepare. What is binding is the assessment made by your tax adviser.

Are the contracts legally watertight?

The templates are carefully built and cite the relevant provisions. They are not legal advice, however, and do not replace review by a lawyer in an individual case. Check the wording before you use it routinely — you can edit everything in the app.
